# CrewFlow Keyword Research

_Market: United Kingdom · Audience: construction SMEs (1–30 staff)_

300+ keywords grouped by **search intent** and **priority**, each mapped to the
page that targets it. Pages marked ✅ are **already built** in this codebase;
🔜 = roadmapped (see `03-content-strategy.md`).

## How to read this

**Intent**
- **C** = Commercial / transactional (ready-to-buy; highest value)
- **X** = Comparison / alternative (high intent, mid-funnel)
- **L** = Local (geo-modified, high intent)
- **N** = Trade/industry (segment intent)
- **I** = Informational (top-funnel, link & authority builder)
- **B** = Brand

**Priority** — P0 (do first / highest ROI) → P3 (long-tail, opportunistic).
Priority blends estimated UK volume × commercial intent × ranking realism for a
new domain. A new site wins long-tail and comparison terms first, then earns the
authority to rank head terms.
