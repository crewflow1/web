# Competitor Analysis (UK Construction Software)

A strategic read on the competitors that matter for CrewFlow's keywords, their
weaknesses, and the gaps CrewFlow attacks. Each comparison page in `/compare`
operationalises this analysis.

## The competitive landscape, segmented

CrewFlow's wedge: **the only all-in-one operating system built for UK
construction SMEs** — UK tax (VAT/PAYE/Corp Tax), UK payroll, bank-transfer
payment tracking, per-job profitability, AND the CRM/jobs/scheduling layer, in
one tool, live in days, transparent pricing. No single competitor occupies that
exact box.

| Competitor | Origin | Built for | CrewFlow's wedge against them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procore | US | Large enterprise GCs | Too big/expensive for SMEs; not UK-tax native |
| Buildertrend | US | US home builders/remodelers | US-shaped; not UK VAT/PAYE/Corp Tax |
| Jobber | CA | Home-service field businesses | No construction job costing, no UK payroll |
| Tradify | NZ | Small trades job management | No payroll/profitability/tax depth |
| Simpro | AU | Larger field-service contractors | Heavy, slow to implement, not UK-tax native |
| ServiceM8 | AU | Micro field-service (iOS) | Light job app, no payroll/costing/tax |
| Buildxact | AU | Estimating-led residential builders | Narrow (estimating), not whole-business |
| Powered Now | UK | UK sole traders / small trades | UK ✓ but quoting/invoicing-only scope |
| Commusoft / Joblogic / BigChange | UK | Field-service/maintenance | Service-ops focus, not construction OS for SMEs |

## Per-competitor profiles

### Procore
- **Position:** Enterprise construction management; the default for large GCs and
  owners. Strong brand, huge content library, deep domain authority.
- **SEO footprint:** Very high DR; ranks for broad "construction management"
  head terms; massive resource/blog library; strong backlink profile from
  industry press and integrations.
- **Weakness for our market:** Enterprise pricing + implementation; overkill and
  unaffordable for SMEs; not built for UK VAT/PAYE/Corp Tax; US-centric content.
- **Opportunity:** Own **"procore alternative uk"** and **"construction software
  for small business uk"**. We will never out-authority Procore on head terms
  soon, but we win the SME-qualifier and alternative long-tail decisively.

### Buildertrend
- **Position:** US residential build/remodel project management + client comms.
- **SEO footprint:** Strong US content; ranks residential PM terms.
- **Weakness:** US tax/payment model; "Buildertrend UK" intent is underserved.
- **Opportunity:** **"buildertrend alternative uk"** + UK-native finance angle.

### Jobber
- **Position:** Field-service management; very strong brand and content machine
  (Jobber Academy). High volume on "jobber" + service-trade terms.
- **Weakness:** Built for home services, not construction; no job costing from
  clocked labour; no UK payroll/tax.
- **Opportunity:** **"jobber alternative uk"**, **"jobber for construction"**, and
  every "construction [job costing/payroll/tax]" term Jobber doesn't target.

### Tradify
- **Position:** Trades job management (quote/schedule/invoice); popular in UK/NZ/AU
  with small trades.
- **Weakness:** Scope stops at job management; payroll, profitability, tax are
  gaps that growing firms fill with spreadsheets.
- **Opportunity:** **"tradify alternative"** + "what to use when you outgrow
  Tradify" (the up-market migration story).

### Simpro
- **Position:** Larger field-service/maintenance contractors; deep, configurable.
- **Weakness:** Complexity, long implementation, not UK-tax native; intimidating
  for SMEs.
- **Opportunity:** **"simpro alternative uk"** + "simpler than Simpro / live in
  days" positioning.

### ServiceM8
- **Position:** Micro field-service, iOS-first, light and loved by sole operators.
- **Weakness:** Too light for a construction firm with staff; no payroll/costing/
  tax; iOS bias.
- **Opportunity:** **"servicem8 alternative"** for firms that have outgrown a job
  app; "works on Android + desktop" angle.

### Buildxact
- **Position:** Estimating + takeoff for residential builders.
- **Weakness:** Narrow; not a whole-business system; not UK-tax native.
- **Opportunity:** **"buildxact alternative"** + "estimating is one part of the
  business" breadth story.

### Powered Now (UK)
- **Position:** UK trades app for quotes + invoices; mobile-first; genuinely UK.
- **Weakness:** Scope is quoting/invoicing; no payroll/profitability/rota depth.
- **Opportunity:** Respect the UK angle (don't claim "more UK"), win on **scope**
  for firms with staff. **"powered now alternative"**.

### Commusoft / Joblogic / BigChange / Eworks (UK field-service)
- **Position:** UK field-service/maintenance management (often heavier, B2B).
- **Weakness:** Service-ops focus rather than construction-SME operating system;
  often enterprise-ish pricing/onboarding.
- **Opportunity:** Build `/compare/crewflow-vs-commusoft` and `-joblogic` (🔜);
  win "construction [vs field service] software" intent.

## Content-gap analysis (where competitors are weak and we attack)

1. **UK-tax-native construction content** — VAT/CIS/PAYE/Corp Tax + construction
   ops in one voice. US tools can't credibly own this. **CrewFlow's biggest moat.**
2. **"All-in-one for UK SMEs"** intent — competitors are either enterprise
   (Procore/Simpro) or single-function (Tradify/Powered Now/Buildxact). The
   "one system, leads-to-tax, for a 1–30 person firm" lane is open.
3. **Honest comparison pages** — most competitor comparison pages are one-sided.
   Our "where they win too" honesty ranks better and earns trust (and links).
4. **Trade-specific + UK-local** combos — few competitors invest in
   "[trade] software UK" + "construction software [city]". We've started both.
5. **Free tools / templates** — quote/invoice templates + markup/day-rate
   calculators are proven link magnets competitors under-invest in for the UK.
6. **Bank-transfer-first** positioning — "no card-processing fees, keep 100% of
   every invoice" is distinctive and true; lean into it.

## Backlink-gap method (do this with a tool)

1. In Ahrefs/Semrush, run **Link Intersect** on procore.com, buildertrend.com,
   jobber.com, tradify.com, simpro.io, servicem8.com, buildxact.com,
   powerednow.com → find domains linking to ≥ 2 of them but not CrewFlow.
2. Filter to UK + construction-relevant, DR-sorted.
3. Those are your priority outreach list — feed them into
   `05-offsite-pr-backlinks-social.md`.
4. Re-run quarterly; track CrewFlow's referring-domain growth vs the cohort.

**KPI:** referring domains and ranking keywords vs the competitor cohort,
tracked monthly. Goal: out-rank every competitor on their own "[brand]
alternative uk" term within 2 quarters, and enter the top 10 for the P0 SME
head terms within 4.
