### The real diagnosis
A young domain (~3 months) with **near-zero backlinks** ranks page 3-7 for
competitive terms **no matter how good the page is**. The content is already strong.
**The bottleneck is off-site authority (backlinks, directory/review presence,
brand citations) + time — not technical SEO and not copy.**

## The plan, by lever and owner

### A. On-page — winnable terms (CODE — in this branch `seo/rank-push-cis-subbie`)
Marginal for competitive heads, decisive for the winnable long-tail. Done here:
1. Sharpen the pricing/costing guide for "how to **cost** construction jobs" / "price a job **as a contractor uk**" (pos 14-17 → page 1).
2. Capture the **subcontractor compliance** intent (insurance/certification/SSIP evidence, pos 12) on the subcontractor-management page — maps to real product (subcontractor mgmt + evidence storage).
3. Internal-linking pass so hub/related/footer flow authority to the money pages with keyword anchors.

### B. Off-site authority — THE lever (FOUNDER / ops; see `05` + `08` + pack below)
This is what moves the competitive heads and earns the knowledge panel:
1. **Directory listings** (consistent NAP, link back): Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, G2, Crunchbase, Product Hunt, Trustpilot + UK construction directories. (Account creation = founder.)
2. **Reviews** — every onboarded customer → Capterra/Google/Trustpilot. Once real reviews exist, switch on review schema (builder ready in `lib/seo/schema.ts`).
3. **Google Business Profile** (Belfast) — NAP + category + posts.
4. **Social/profile handles** — claim the exact handles in `05` so schema `sameAs`
   resolves (cements the entity → de-dilutes the crowded "CrewFlow" name → knowledge panel).
5. **Backlinks/PR** — trade associations (CEF/FMB), NI business press, construction
   SaaS roundups, guest posts (`08` target list + templates). Outreach can be sent
   from hello@crewflow.uk (same pipeline as the sales campaign) on approval.

### C. Measurement (weekly)
GSC impressions/clicks/avg-position; indexed count; referring domains; positions for
the P0 terms above; demo conversions.
