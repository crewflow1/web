15. Crozdesk / SaaSworthy / SourceForge listings.
16. Press coverage (see PR below) — aim for 2–3 ranking articles.

panel. Claim it via Google once it appears.

**identical everywhere**; cross-link profiles back to `crewflow.uk`; use the same
logo (`/icon.svg`) and OG image everywhere for visual brand consistency.

- **Additional categories:** Business Management Consultant; Computer Software

- **Service area:** Belfast, Lisburn, Newry, Northern Ireland (+ "United Kingdom"

- **Description (750 chars):**
  > CrewFlow is the all-in-one operating system for UK construction companies.
  > Built in Belfast, CrewFlow brings leads, quotes, jobs, scheduling, staff
  > timesheets, payroll, invoicing, payments, profitability and tax (VAT, PAYE,
  > Corporation Tax) into one system — so builders, electricians, plumbers,
  > roofers and trades across the UK can run the whole business from one screen.
  > Replace five disconnected apps and spreadsheets. Live in days. Book a demo.
- **Services (list each, with a sentence):** Construction CRM, Quoting &
  Estimating, Job Management, Scheduling & Rota, Timesheets, Payroll, Invoicing,
  Payment Tracking, Job Costing, Tax & VAT, AI Receptionist, Customer Portal.
- **Photos required:** logo, brand banner, 4–6 product screenshots (dashboard,
  quote, invoice, rota, payroll, job costing), 1 founder/team photo, 1 "built in
  Belfast" graphic. (Reuse the dynamic OG cards as a start.)

  Ireland?" "Does it handle UK VAT and payroll?" "How long to get set up?" "Does

  (These also seed future, *genuine* review schema.)
