# Backlink Strategy — 250 Targets, Templates & Campaigns

Extends the 100 opportunities in `05-offsite-pr-backlinks-social.md` to **250**,
with outreach templates, guest-post angles and digital-PR campaigns. This is the
core of Phase 2: authority.

> Targets 1–100 are in `05`. This doc adds **101–250** plus the playbook.

---

## Prioritisation: score every target (Authority × Ease)

For each prospect, score **Authority** (1–5, est. domain rating / relevance) and
**Ease** (1–5, how gettable). Work the **high-authority × high-ease** quadrant
first. A CIOB or Construction News link (high authority, low ease — earn via PR)
is worth 50 directory links; a Capterra listing (mid authority, high ease — do
today) is free money. Track every live link, its DR, and anchor in a sheet.

**Sequence:** (1) all high-ease listings/directories now → (2) trade-association
+ review profiles → (3) guest posts + digital PR (the DR-movers) over months →
(4) relationships (podcasts, partners) ongoing.

## Outreach templates

**Guest post pitch**
> Subject: Guest piece for [site]: [specific, useful angle]
> Hi [name] — I'm [founder] at CrewFlow (we build software for UK construction
> firms). I'd love to write a genuinely useful piece for [site]'s readers:
> "[title]" — practical, no pitch, [1-line of what they'll learn]. I can include
> original examples and a custom graphic. Recent example of my writing: [link].
> Useful? — [name]

**Resource-page / listicle add**
> Subject: A free [calculator/guide] for your [page title] resource list
> Hi [name] — your page [URL] is a great roundup. We just published a free
> [markup & margin calculator / construction guide] with no sign-up that your
> readers would find useful: [link]. Might be worth adding alongside the others.
> Either way, nice resource. — [name]

**Broken-link**
> Subject: Broken link on [page]
> Hi [name] — quick heads up, the link to [dead resource] on [URL] is 404ing. If
> you need a replacement, our [free tool/guide] covers the same ground: [link].
> — [name]

**Digital-PR / data**
> Subject: Data: [headline stat] across UK construction
> Hi [name] — we analysed [dataset] and found [surprising stat]. Full breakdown +
> regional data + charts here: [link]. Happy to give [publication] the exclusive
> and a quote. — [name]

- "Building a construction SaaS from Belfast" (founder story) → NI/startup media.
- "What to look for in construction software in 2026" → buyer-intent media.

## Digital-PR campaign concepts (the DR movers)

1. **"State of UK construction admin" survey** — poll 200+ firms on hours lost to
   admin, late payment, tool sprawl. Publish a report with regional cuts +
   charts. Pitch national + trade press. (Repeatable annually = a link annuity.)
2. **Late-payment index** — analyse average days-to-pay by trade/region. Highly
   linkable; ties to your invoicing/get-paid-faster content.
3. **Free interactive tools as link bait** — the `/tools` calculators; expand to
   a profit calculator, day-rate calculator, CIS calculator, roofing/paint
   calculators (architecture is ready — `06`), then outreach each to listicles.
4. **"Cost to build" data** — regional £/m² extension/loft data; perennial press
   bait.

**KPI:** referring domains up and to the right, month over month, with quarterly
Link-Intersect refills (`04`). One Tier-1 editorial link (Construction News,
CIOB, UKTN) > 50 directories — spend PR effort accordingly.
